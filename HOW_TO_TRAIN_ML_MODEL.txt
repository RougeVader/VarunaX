1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The VarunX Early Warning System uses a multi-class machine learning risk engine
(XGBoost + RandomForest) trained on 10 physical and meteorological parameters
to classify real-time catchment hazard into 4 operational alert levels:
  - Level 0: Low Risk (Green)
  - Level 1: Medium Risk (Yellow)
  - Level 2: High Risk (Orange)
  - Level 3: Critical Risk (Red) — Urgent Evacuation

This guide provides all prerequisites, environment setup instructions, and
commands to train and export the models locally utilizing your NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU.

Step 2: (Optional) Activate Virtual Environment

Step 3: Run the Training Script
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Execute the automated training script:
  python train_model.py

What this script does automatically:
  1. Generates the realistic historical flash-flood & landslide synthetic dataset 
     and saves it to: data/historical_varunx_data.csv
  2. Detects your NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU and enables CUDA acceleration (tree_method="hist", device="cuda").
  3. Trains the primary XGBoost Classifier and secondary RandomForest Classifier.
  4. Evaluates accuracy, multi-class ROC-AUC, confusion matrix, and feature importances.
  5. Exports all model binaries and evaluation metrics to the models/ folder.

5. GENERATED ARTIFACTS & OUTPUTS
================================================================================
After training completes, the following files will be created/updated:

1. Dataset:
   - data/historical_varunx_data.csv (2,500 samples with 10 PRD sensor columns)

2. Model Weights & Feature Registry:
   - models/varunx_risk_model.pkl     -> Primary XGBoost classifier

   - models/feature_cols.pkl          -> Serialized list of 10 input feature names

3. Performance Metrics:
   - models/metrics.json              -> Accuracy, ROC-AUC, Confusion Matrix, 
                                         and Feature Importances for the ML Studio tab.


================================================================================
6. FEATURE SET & PHYSICAL PARAMETERS
================================================================================
The models are trained strictly on the 10 VarunX physical parameters:

1.  rainfall_1h_mm       : 1-hour rainfall intensity (mm)
2.  rainfall_3h_mm       : 3-hour cumulative precipitation (mm)
3.  rainfall_24h_mm      : 24-hour cumulative precipitation (mm)
4.  flow_water_level_m   : River / valley gauge water depth (meters)
5.  water_level_rate_m_h : Rate of change of water level (m/hour)
6.  slope_movement_mm    : Hillside displacement / inclinometer reading (mm)
7.  tilt_rate_mm_h       : Rate of ground displacement (mm/hour)
8.  discharge_m3s        : Water discharge rate (m³/s)
9.  air_temp_c           : Ambient air temperature (°C)
10. surface_temp_c       : Soil / surface temperature (°C)
